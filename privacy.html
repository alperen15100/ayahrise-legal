Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Overview

AyahRise is designed to work primarily on-device. The app does not require an AyahRise account and Ecrin Labs does not operate an analytics or advertising backend for AyahRise.

Data stored on your device

Reading progress, bookmarks, collections, notes, reading-plan progress, preferences, reminder settings and downloaded recitation files may be stored locally on your Android device. AyahRise also provides export/import tools so you can create your own backup file.

Network access

Internet access may be used to stream or download Quran recitation audio and to open source, support or legal webpages. When your device connects to a third-party website or media host, that provider may receive ordinary network information such as your IP address and request metadata according to its own policies.

Notifications and background audio

If you enable reading reminders, AyahRise may use Android notification functionality to show local reminders. When recitation audio is playing, Android foreground media playback features may be used so playback can continue while the app is not in the foreground.

Advertising and analytics

AyahRise does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in the current release.

Sensitive permissions

AyahRise does not require access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, SMS messages or call logs for its core Quran reading features.

Sharing

If you choose to share an ayah or exported backup, Android's system sharing interface or another app selected by you handles that action. AyahRise does not automatically upload your notes or reading history to Ecrin Labs.

Support communications

If you contact us by email, the information you send is processed by the email services involved in delivering and receiving that message. We use support messages only to respond to your request and maintain support records when necessary.

Children

AyahRise is a general-audience Quran reading application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an AyahRise account system because the app does not provide such an account system in the current release.

Changes

We may update this policy when app functionality changes. The latest version will always be published at this URL.
